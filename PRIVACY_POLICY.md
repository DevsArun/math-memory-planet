# Privacy Policy — Math Memory Planet

**Effective date:** 10 August 2026
**Developer:** Devs Arun — itsdevsarun@gmail.com

## The short version
Math Memory Planet collects **no data at all**. No personal information, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts, no internet access. Everything stays on your child's device.

## What the app does NOT do
- Does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information
- Does not require an account or login
- Does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs
- Does not access the internet (the app declares zero network permissions)
- Does not access the camera, microphone, contacts, files or location
- Does not contain in-app purchases or any external links

## What stays on the device
Game progress (stars, levels, rewards, settings) is stored **locally on the device only**, using the operating system's standard app storage. It is never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it completely.

## Children's privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)
This app is designed for children ages 3–12. Because the app collects zero data and works fully offline, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR requirements for children by design: there is simply nothing to collect.

## Third parties
None. The app integrates no third-party services.
