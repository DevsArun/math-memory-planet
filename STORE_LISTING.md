## Long description
Math Memory Planet turns math practice into a space adventure kids actually ask to play.

🪐 7 PLANETS, 7 DIFFERENT GAMES
• Number Pairs — classic memory matching with numbers
• Equation Match — match 4 + 5 to 9
• Rocket Sequence — watch and repeat the growing pattern
• Hide and Seek — memorize, then find the hidden number
• Target Sum — make pairs that add up to the target
• Small to Big — tap cards in ascending order from memory
• Build the Equation — rebuild 4 + 5 = 9 in the right order

⭐ MADE FOR AGES 3–12
Three difficulty bands on every planet: gentle for ages 3–5, growing for 6–8, challenging for 9–12. 210 unique levels — every level is different, always solvable, always fair.

🏆 STARS, STREAKS AND REWARDS
Kids earn up to 3 stars per level, keep a daily streak, and unlock card backs, galaxy themes and confetti styles. All rewards are earned by playing — nothing costs money, ever.

🛡️ 100% KID-SAFE
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no internet
• No data collection of any kind — progress stays on the device
• Parental gate protects Settings and the parent stats screen
• Works fully offline — perfect for Fire Tablets at home or on the go

🌍 7 LANGUAGES
English, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português, Italiano, 日本語.

One-time purchase. All content unlocked from the first tap.
